## What this app does

RailSathi shows train running status, seat availability and PNR status. It gets that information
from a railway data service configured by the app's publisher.

## Data stored on your device

* Your favourite trains.
* Your train search history.
* The last successful running status for trains you looked up, so it can be shown offline, marked
  with its update time.

This data stays on your device. You can remove favourites and clear history inside the app, or
clear everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings. The app does not back up this
data to the cloud (`allowBackup` is off).

## Data sent over the network

The train numbers, station codes, dates and PNR numbers you enter are sent over HTTPS to the data
service to get results. Your PNR is sent in the request body, is never saved on your device, and is
never written to logs.

## What the app does not collect

No account, name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, advertising ID or analytics.
The app requests only the Internet and network-state permissions.

## Passenger information

PNR results are held in memory while you view them and discarded afterwards. They are not saved or
cached.

## Data providers

The data service the app connects to has its own terms and privacy practices. The publisher is
responsible for choosing a provider whose terms permit this use, and for describing any server-side
logging or retention in its own privacy notice.

## Sample data mode

Builds without a configured data service show clearly labelled sample data that does not describe
real trains or bookings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
